About the laboratory

Power electronics for a more efficient and electrified world.

The Power Electronics Laboratory at Nantong University brings together research in power conversion, control, and energy systems. Our work connects fundamental engineering questions with practical challenges in modern electrification.

We pursue rigorous research, hands-on experimentation, and collaboration across disciplines.
